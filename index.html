What is
ZATCA E-Invoicing

This new resolution aims to eliminate all paper-based and handwritten invoices by implementing E-invoicing software compatible with ZATCA. 

It requires all VAT-registered taxpayers and their representatives in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to comply.  Invoices must be transmitted electronically through the ZATCA platform, ensuring accuracy, transparency, and compliance with tax regulations. 

The e-invoicing software will facilitate real-time tracking and reporting, streamlining financial management for businesses while allowing government agencies to monitor compliance. Non-compliance may result in penalties or fines.

Two Phases Of
ZATCA

Generation

Businesses must generate and store e-invoices in a structured format with mandatory fields, including a QR code, VAT number, and digital timestamp. Paper invoices are no longer valid without digital compliance.

E-invoicing applies to all persons subject to VAT and any other parties issuing tax invoices on behalf of suppliers subject to VAT. Non-resident taxable persons for VAT purposes are excluded.

All the data fields that are visible on human readable form of the invoice must be in Arabic. VAT Regulations require the Tax Invoices and Simplified Tax Invoices (along with corresponding credit notes or debit notes) to be in Arabic mandatorily. Invoice can be bilingual and include English as well.

The technical requirements are typically implemented by solution providers (e.g. cash register vendors, software vendors) or the internal technical teams for in-house built solutions. Taxpayers may approach a solution provider or their internal technical teams to acquire or upgrade to a compliant electronic system; and make sure they are generating compliant invoices.
